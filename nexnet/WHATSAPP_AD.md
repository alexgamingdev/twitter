*What NexNet offers*

*Neural Feed* — A real-time global broadcast stream. See what every active Node is posting, live, without algorithmic interference or curated timelines.

*Secure Comms* — Direct messaging with real encryption. No third party reads your messages. No messages are stored against your will.

*Verified Node Status* — Apply for official verification and receive a cryptographic badge that authenticates your identity within the network. Verified status is issued directly through the Firestore database and cannot be faked.

*Bot Protection* — Powered by Cloudflare Turnstile, NexNet ensures that only verified human nodes can access the network. No bots, no spam, no pollution of the stream.

*Node Registry* — Full control over your digital profile. Your data lives where you decide it lives. No silent background processes, no cloud mining.
